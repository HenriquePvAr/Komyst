## Languages

- **Website** — Portuguese, English, Spanish, Japanese, Korean and Russian.
- **App interface** — Brazilian Portuguese, English, Spanish, French, Japanese and Korean in the inspected catalog.
- **Advertised OCR and translation source languages** — English, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Russian, Spanish and French. The published chapter benchmarks document English, Korean and Russian. Language recognition or selection in the app does not guarantee equivalent results for other languages.
- **Documented destination for the demonstrated workflows** — Brazilian Portuguese.
